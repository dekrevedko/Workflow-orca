# Protocol requirements and verification review

## What the existing requirements settle

* **Sequential waits.** EV-043 and AC-109 allow the next occurrence to use the same signal stream after the first event is consumed. EV-011 and AC-107 require rejection of simultaneous active owners, including within one instance. The corrected ephemeral path accepts the event into the blocked fiber on its execution lane before downstream code can activate another wait. Internally the old reservation stays held until the continuation settles. A duplicate event cannot advance a same-key successor during the retained deduplication window. [Event requirements](../../docs/specs/05-requirements-events-waits-timers.md) and [acceptance criteria](../../docs/specs/12-acceptance-criteria.md)
* **Event identity.** EV-003/020 and AC-103 require the contract name, version, and correlation ID to match. EV-010/011/013 and AC-107 scope the unique active key by definition. Under EV-043, a later wait can reuse that stream after consumption. [Event requirements](../../docs/specs/05-requirements-events-waits-timers.md), [acceptance criteria](../../docs/specs/12-acceptance-criteria.md), [canonical event identity](../../openspec/specs/event-routing-and-waits/spec.md), and [routing index](../../src/OrcaCore.Engine.Ephemeral/Execution/EphemeralRoutingIndex.cs)
* **Ephemeral caller cancellation.** The product requirements establish observable ordering and uniqueness. The corrected implementation chooses a specific boundary. Before event acceptance, cancellation can leave the predecessor available for retry. Once accepted into the continuation, the instance drives the turn and returns a receipt even if the caller cancels. An exception in user code after acceptance can fail the workflow. This decision concerns event ingress through the process-local bridge and is distinct from workflow cancellation. [Current checks](wait-ownership/README.md#current-implementation)
* **Durable matching and driving.** EV-032 couples wait consumption, inbox progression and the resulting transition. DR-034 preserves runnable work across host loss and gives the continuation pump a separate claim and disposition. The durable model and C# cases cover both sides of the match commit and one committed resume. Qualification of individual SQL backends still requires provider tests. [Durable driver requirements](../../docs/specs/16-requirements-durable-driver.md) and [model scope](durable-wait-handoff/README.md)

The [continuation lease checks](durable-continuation-lease/README.md) exposed an extra poll when an expired worker reopened a dispatched record. The current provider contract fences mark, poison and release by the acquired attempt. The replacement worker keeps its active lease or its dispatched result, and the stale caller receives false.

The [durable event versus timeout checks](durable-event-timeout/README.md) distinguish occurrence ownership from what prompts delivery. A's late timeout leaves B active. Under EV-043 and EV-051, a direct redelivery of applied E1 can prompt a match of a separately accepted E2. The journal identifies E2 as B's winner and retains A's original E1 match. This distinction matters when interpreting a duplicate ingress acknowledgement.

The [ephemeral start-key retirement checks](start-key-retirement/README.md) confirm the canonical replay boundary. A pin preserves the inspected binding while the caller captures its decision. Once the pin is released, retirement and reuse can precede return of the original handle or conflict. A later ordinary lookup through that handle addresses the original instance ID. Elapsed age and the time at which a caller enters the API do not alone determine the result. [Canonical retention requirement](../../openspec/specs/management-and-querying/spec.md#requirement-ephemeral-start-idempotency-expires-with-atomic-retirement)

The DAG child join uses the winning child cancellation request as required by CP-023/024. Both first delivery and redelivery preserve an independent cancellation across a later parent barrier. A genuinely changed terminal output still faults the run. Both orderings passed after killing the original worker and recovering from PostgreSQL or SQL Server. A legacy first join after the barrier may lack enough evidence to classify the child and requires reconciliation. [DAG child join checks](dag-child-join/README.md) and [composition requirements](../../docs/specs/08-requirements-composition.md#cp-023-one-durable-child-instance-per-node-occurrence)

MG-064 and the durable lexical lease contract allow a trusted integration to confirm that protected work has stopped or that the resource has fenced its token. An empty job lookup can precede the first submission. Checking the provider's single live ticket alone misses simultaneous external use. The SQL schedules observe the protected resource independently and expose two gaps in how the durable driver restores the waiter's lease context after a grant. [Late protected step evidence](late-protected-step-lease-release/README.md) and [canonical lease requirement](../../openspec/specs/durable-runtime/spec.md#requirement-durable-leases-are-lexical-occurrence-owned-obligations)
