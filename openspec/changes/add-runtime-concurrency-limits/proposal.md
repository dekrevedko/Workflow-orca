This change owns per-step execution throttles and named cross-instance transient pools; it
does not implement or imply persisted durable resource leases.

## Why

Hosts need predictable upper bounds on structured paths within an instance, exact configured step types across a host, and shared infrastructure such as database connections or pooled HTTP clients. The archived fiber work delivered useful mechanics for the ephemeral engine, but some provisional advancement/general-body options are not part of the approved v1 surface; the durable engine also lacks equivalent transient governance and restart re-admission. Operators and authors need one explicit cross-mode contract for caps such as “no more than four concurrent database-backed operations across this host” without confusing host-local limits with durable leases.

## What Changes

- Reuse the delivered ephemeral **runtime resource governance** mechanics while retargeting the public surface to two transient categories: execution throttles keyed only by named `Then<TStep>()` step types and held only around the actual step body, and named host-local cross-instance pools shared by multiple steps or workflows. Lambda steps have no exact-type throttle target. One ephemeral step may select at most one named pool. Registration reports every missing authored transient pool through the closed host-compatibility result before mutation; configured-pool saturation still parks at runtime. Forced logical termination cancels pending admission but never frees a granted slot before that guarded body stops.
- Complete durable host-owned path/step governance, blocked-owner restart re-admission, cross-mode verification, and operator configuration. Named `WithTransientPool` authoring remains ephemeral-only in the first release; durable named-pool authoring requires a future matrix amendment even after host enforcement exists.
- Carry shared path/step limits through `StructuredExecutionHostOptions`, nested in the exact role-specific `EphemeralEngineHostOptions` and `DurableEngineHostOptions`. Registration occurs through `AddOrcaCoreEphemeralEngine(...)` or `AddOrcaCoreDurableEngine(...)`; this change does not introduce a catch-all `AddOrcaCore`, separate hosted-service toggle, or implicit mode selection.
- Make per-instance execution-path scheduling a host-owned safety limit named `MaxConcurrentExecutionPathsPerInstance`. A runnable root, branch, or item owns one countable token; it releases the token before parking on a wait, delay, resource request, or join. A fan-out parent releases its token before child scheduling and reacquires one only for merge/continuation, so path-token capacity alone cannot create a parent-held-token deadlock at a ceiling of one. Workflow definitions do not carry an author-global path cap. Every fixed root-`Parallel` branch fiber exists at scope start and runnable branches receive tokens fairly in authored order; there is no separate live-fiber admission resource. Root `ForEach` alone may declare a positive node-local `maxConcurrency`, whose effective admitted-item ceiling is the lower of that value and the host ceiling; parking retains that item slot, so global progress is not promised when admitted items depend on pending ones.
- Use `TransientPoolName` for named host-local admission and reserve distinct `ResourcePoolName` plus **durable resource lease** for the separate persisted cross-host/fiber-occurrence capability with atomic queueing, lexical ownership, `AmbiguousHeld` retry retention, quarantine transfer, deterministic release, and owner-state reconciliation. Lease authoring is available at the durable root, root-nested `If`/`While` bodies, and independent durable root-`Parallel` branch/root-`ForEach` item bodies only when no live ancestor lease exists; dedicated leased builders expose no fan-out, nested acquisition, or `ContinueAsNew`. A durable review deadline marks ownership for reconciliation; it is not an expiry, TTL, or time-only reclaim. This change does not implement those leases, make transient pools restart-durable, or add lease renewal.
- Record how these limits **compose** with existing **one logical mutator per instance** semantics (they address different concerns: correctness ordering versus operational capacity).
- Make saturation park the exact requesting owner with cancellation after releasing the instance turn. V1 has no independent host-wide advancement ceiling, fail-fast saturation mode, capacity-wait timeout, stacked named pools, throttle scope beyond exact step type, or custom transient-governance SPI. Driver segment budgets remain separate fairness mechanics.
- Capture implementation-facing scenarios for configuration, observability, cooperative local-fiber blocking, and true concurrent external or cross-instance work.
- Maintain the normative specifications and focused design for the delivered ephemeral baseline and the remaining durable implementation.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `runtime-resource-governance`: Host-owned per-instance path admission, node-local root-`ForEach` bounds, configurable per-step throttles, and named cross-instance transient pools, composable with per-instance serialization and cooperative fiber turns and distinct from durable resource leases.

## Impact

- The archived structured-fiber work already delivered bounded admission mechanics, named transient pools, cooperative blocked obligations, and focused regressions. This change accepts those mechanics without freezing provisional advancement/general-body options and owns their convergence on the exact path/step/pool contract plus durable restart re-admission and cross-mode verification.
- Durable paths share the host-owned governance contract where both modes claim support. Durable named transient-pool authoring stays absent for v1 and is not unlocked automatically by implementation evidence.
- The archived `adopt-structured-fiber-execution` baseline is authoritative for local composition: local paths execute cooperatively, and a saturated local fiber commits or records its owned blocked obligation and releases the instance turn before any wait for capacity. A granted physical step-throttle/transient slot remains counted until its guarded body actually returns, even after the logical attempt is timed out or fenced.
- `reshape-developer-facing-interfaces` is authoritative for public naming and mode-guaranteed discoverability. Static builders do not gain methods from later host composition: ephemeral transient-pool authoring is delivered, while durable transient-pool authoring is deferred from v1. All three artifacts use the same per-step throttle / named transient pool / durable lease taxonomy.
