## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ephemeral native support requires six successful native cells
Issue #13 support SHALL require external PackageReference-only candidate consumers executing self-contained untrimmed JIT, full-trim JIT and Native AOT on native Windows Server 2025 win-x64 and Ubuntu 24.04 linux-x64 under .NET 10. Trimmed/AOT artifacts SHALL assert SDK-default disabled JSON reflection. Every AOT-01–16 scenario from the immutable issue #3 task-2 protocol SHALL retain its assertions and succeed, with NS-01–12 metadata/bootstrap/parity/closed-dispatch cases and mandatory F1/P3 subcases from the R1 protocol added. F1 root/nested omitted/present shapes SHALL be expected negative assertions in every Generated-mode cell, with unconfigured legacy JIT retained as the separate unchanged-behavior control; no original positive scenario SHALL be waived. P3 SHALL include rejection/retry controls for the baked enum policy and handwritten renamed-property context. Generated JIT and unconfigured legacy JIT SHALL be controls. Candidate source/package/harness hashes, commands, environment and outcomes SHALL bind the evidence. Cross-publication, WSL/containers, a successful publish, a skipped/blocked case or Unsupported result SHALL NOT complete this support issue. Missing native execution SHALL keep the target incomplete.

#### Scenario: Native publication succeeds but one workflow fails
- **WHEN** a published artifact cannot execute any required positive journey or expected negative behavior
- **THEN** support qualification fails, the exact failure remains recorded and no support claim is published for that cell

#### Scenario: New evidence supersedes an earlier unsupported version
- **WHEN** the new candidate passes every required cell and receives independent approval
- **THEN** the claim names the qualified source/package/profile and preserves issue #3's original unsupported records and old released-version wording as dated history

### Requirement: Native support diagnostics and public transitions remain truthful
The implementation target SHALL enumerate every generator/trim/AOT diagnostic individually, remediate OrcaCore-attributable warnings on supported paths and reject unexpected or unclassified findings. Third-party/toolchain findings SHALL require exact supported-path safety and independent approval. Blanket suppressions, private or whole-assembly rooting and reflection-enabling switches SHALL NOT establish compatibility. API/compile/package baselines SHALL change together with their source after contract approval. Package-wide IsAotCompatible/IsTrimmable metadata or extra public annotations SHALL require a reviewed whole-exposed-surface assessment and catalogue amendment; selected journey success alone SHALL NOT justify them. Required CI paths SHALL remain green, and ordinary dotnet test evidence SHALL NOT be replaced by direct-runner-only evidence. Owned temporary/cache/build output SHALL be removed after retaining compact validation evidence.

#### Scenario: A downstream warning remains after first-error remediation
- **WHEN** a newly reachable codec, delegate, generic, event or failure path emits a diagnostic
- **THEN** qualification requires its individual disposition and supported-path remedy rather than accepting the old assessment baseline or suppressing the category

#### Scenario: Only selected ephemeral packages have native evidence
- **WHEN** documentation or package metadata describes compatibility
- **THEN** it states the exact finite native recipe and closure without claiming durable/DAG/provider or twelve-package compatibility
